# Example kickoff prompt — Soteri Skin "Eczema, the pH villain"

Paste this into an agent that has the `animated-explainer-ad` skill loaded. The skill walks you through phases 1–8 from here.

---

Make an animated explainer ad for **Soteri Skin** — their **Baby Eczema Relief Cream**, the one "Powered by pH/LOCK® Technology, pH 4.9."

**Format:** absurdist Pixar 3D, villain narrates own defeat, same pattern as the HUM "Big Chill" ad. 9:16, ~38s, paid social.

**The villain is Eczema** — a small knee-high cartoon gremlin with rough sandpapery hot coral-red skin covered in tiny soft spikes, a frizzy dark-red tuft of bristles, big mischievous eyes, and a smug jagged grin. Pesky and funny, never genuinely scary — this is a baby product.

**The hero is Soteri** — visually the opposite of Eczema. Smooth plump rounded cream-white, gently glowing, with a soft eucalyptus-green (#2E6F5E) cape and a round green padlock crest on the chest reading "pH" (the pH/LOCK).

**The mechanism:** eczema is *really a pH problem*. Healthy baby skin sits slightly acidic at pH 4.9. When Eczema pushes the pH up, the moisture barrier cracks, water escapes, skin goes dry/red/itchy. Soteri's pH/LOCK snaps the pH back to 4.9 and the barrier seals shut.

**Roster (4 — at the cap):**
- Eczema (villain)
- Soteri (hero)
- The baby (~12 months, chubby cheeks, cream onesie — the human victim)
- The moisture barrier (a wall of rounded soft peachy-tan skin-cell bricks, each a tiny plump character with a simple cute face)

**Recurring motif:** "pH 4.9" — appears in the teach beat (scene 5), the damage beat (scene 6), the payoff (scene 10).

**Voice:** villain narrates throughout including the defeat. Try Austin (`Bj9UqZbhQsanLzgalpEG`) first — Texan, raspy, characterful, can do whispers and defeated lines. eleven_v3, ≤4 audio tags total.

**Music:** whimsical Pixar/Disney instrumental — pizzicato strings, light woodwinds, xylophone. Sneaky for the villain intro → light tension through damage → warm uplift at hero arrival → soft resolved tail. NOT cinematic, NOT moody.

**End card:** real product photo at `./inputs/soteri-product-endcard.jpg`. Brand colours: primary eucalyptus green `#2E6F5E`, accent sage `#E3EDE8`, CTA coral `#E8674C`. Wordmark "Soteri Skin", subline "Baby Eczema Relief Cream", claims "Powered by pH/LOCK® Technology" and "Steroid-Free · Fragrance-Free · Ages 0–5", CTA "soteriskin.com".

**Hard constraints:**
- No cure claims. Eczema-the-character can pack his bags and leave ("nowhere left to live") as a story device; product copy stays "helps correct and lock" / "helps repair the barrier."
- Never mention the brand name in any keyframe prompt (nano-banana will hallucinate "SOTERY SKINS" signage). Brand text lives only on the PIL-composited end card and burned captions.
