# Example kickoff prompt — Aurelia "Comeback"

Paste this into an agent that has the `create-cinematic-music-video` skill loaded.
The skill walks you through the pipeline from here. (This is the fictional brand
shipped in `examples/concept.json` + `examples/lyrics-locked.md` — copy those two
files into your run folder to start from a working concept.)

---

Make a cinematic music-video ad for **Aurelia**, a running-apparel brand. The
campaign line is **"every morning is a comeback."**

**Format:** 9:16, ~28s, cinematic music video — an original anthem with real
sung vocals, 14 beat-locked tableaux, burned word-synced captions. Paid social
(TikTok / Reels / Shorts).

**Look pack:** `KODAK_PORTRA_DAY` — warm 35mm golden-hour film, handheld,
grainy, A24-indie. The runner is a woman in her thirties; the hero garment is a
cardinal-red running jersey.

**The arc:** a single morning run as a small act of self-renewal —
anticipation (laces, quiet street) → energy (the run, the hill, the crest) →
reflection (walking it out at twilight). Not a feature montage; an emotional arc.

**Music:** indie-pop anthem at 120 BPM, confident-warm female lead vocal,
cinematic strings, a big stadium drum at the chorus, stripped acoustic outro.
Original — generated by ElevenLabs (no licensed track needed).

**Lyrics:** already written in `source/lyrics-locked.md`. The chorus hook is
"This is my comeback, every morning."

**End card:** wordmark **AURELIA**, tagline "every morning is a comeback", URL
"aurelia.run". Composited in post — do NOT ask the image model to render the
wordmark (it can't draw clean type). The END_CARD tableau is just a clean warm
backdrop.

**Hard constraints:**
- Object-hero / scene framing in keyframe prompts ("Hero subject: a jersey on a
  chair…"), not "a young woman who…" — keeps the image model out of NSFW
  false-flags and off hallucinated faces.
- One look pack across all 14 tableaux — the through-line is what makes it read
  as one ad.
- Captions land on vocal beats (the word timestamps drive the timeline) — never
  hard-code timings.

Output to `./projects/aurelia-comeback/`.

Walk me through it — pause at the storyboard gate and the T01 keyframe gate
before spending on the full batch.
